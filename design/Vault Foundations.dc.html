01 Laws of the direction Non-negotiable
LAW 01 Silence is health Muted means complete. Color belongs to exceptions only — partial, critical, success. A calm board is a healthy dossier; the eye goes straight to what needs work.
LAW 02 Hairlines are the structure, soft fills are the depth 1px lines organize the page; planes of soft fill lift what matters. No gradients, no glow, no glassmorphism — shadows barely there, and only under things that float.
LAW 03 Thin numerals, calm titles Display figures are Geist 200 — large, light, unmissable. Titles stay 600 at modest sizes. Hierarchy comes from scale and space, never from weight noise.
LAW 04 Data is mono, amounts right-aligned — always Codes, references and every peso live in Geist Mono with tabular figures. The UI face never carries data; the mono face never speaks.

05 Geometry One line each
Spacing Base 8 — gutters 56 · module gap 48 · card padding 24 · controls 34/40 · rows 42–48.
Radius 999 controls & pills · 18 cards · 12 bands & menus · 10 list rows · 0 inside tables.
Hairline & shadow 1px at 8% (light) / 10% (dark) ink; faint 5% / 6%. Depth is a soft fill; shadow exists only under the toast.
06 Motion 150–200ms · ease-out · nothing bounces, nothing glows
One curve: cubic-bezier(.25, 0, .2, 1). Fades 150ms, movement 200ms, state resolution 300ms. Two signature moments only — everything else is instant or a plain fade.
